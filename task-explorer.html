The whole test,
down to its moving parts.

The public base is 120 items and about 90 minutes before directions. Reading and Listening use a routing module and an adjusted second module, so delivered time and item counts can vary. Writing and Speaking remain linear.

THE COMPLETION TEST

Underline the verbs

If the prompt says describe, explain, and request, your draft needs visible evidence of all three actions. A polished response that skips one action is incomplete.

04 / INDEPENDENT SPEAKING STUDIO

Answer first.
Build while speaking.

In the 2026 test, the independent-style task is Take an Interview: four related questions, no preparation time in official samples. These response engines keep organization light enough to sound conversational.

ETS BAND-5 TARGET On-topic elaboration+conversational pace+intelligibility+accurate, flexible language Rubric ↗
LISTEN & REPEAT / CHUNK MAP

Store meaning groups, not loose words.

ETS scores exactness and intelligibility. Hear the sentence once, hold its stressed content words and grammar links, then reproduce the original order. If one word disappears, keep moving.
